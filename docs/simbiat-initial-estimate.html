Scope assumptions & proposed terms
The basis for this estimate

A clear launch scope.
A practical schedule.

Custom website with Stripe payments

The website will use Stripe for payments. Product and content editing, inventory, and order records are separate parts of the build. The proposed setup uses Vercel hosting, Contentful for product/editorial content, Neon Free for private store data, and Gmail for order/contact email. Supabase Free is a database alternative. Hosting and CMS account plans still need confirmation.

Stripe Checkout collects payment and delivery details using its supported layout. The website confirms an order only after verifying payment with Stripe. One protected owner screen covers orders and stock; refunds use the Stripe dashboard. Customer/order information stays out of the public content system. Protected owner sign-in and database access controls are included.

Launch assumptions & your materials

  • One US store, USD pricing, guest checkout, one stock location and one owner. Manual packing, shipping, and fulfillment updates. Confirm states served, stock rules, flat shipping rate, and Stripe tax configuration before the final quote.
  • You supply the logo, brand choices, product photos, accurate prices and variants, final recipes/articles, business details, and approved shipping, returns, privacy, and terms text. Formatting up to four policy pages is included.
  • You own the domain and service accounts, pay provider bills directly, and complete payment-provider verification. Required editing access and account roles will be confirmed before selecting the plan.
  • Two rounds of consolidated feedback cover the agreed design and functionality. Extra content, redesigns, or new requirements are priced before work starts.

Timing & payment proposal

Customer information & handoff

Card entry stays with the payment provider. Outside this estimate

Historical Shopify data migration; paid recipes or digital downloads; subscriptions; customer accounts; loyalty or wholesale features; carrier-rate integrations; custom tax logic; multiple currencies/languages; advanced inventory systems; newsletter or marketing automation; content writing, photography, legal drafting, and ongoing campaigns. Other exclusions in the reviewed scope continue to apply.
