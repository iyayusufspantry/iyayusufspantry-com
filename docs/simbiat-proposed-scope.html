03 · Security & support

Care for the information.
Plan for the upkeep.

Your concern includes customer names, addresses, and order information as well as card details. The production plan should address all of these from the start.

Proposed security approach

  • Use an established payment provider. Stripe was discussed as an option, with Shopify also mentioned. Final selection, fees, and integration are subject to review. Full card details should be processed by the provider and not stored by the custom application.
  • Collect only necessary customer information. Agree what is required for purchases, fulfillment, and inquiries, who may access it, and how long it needs to be retained.
  • Restrict customer and administrative access. Plan appropriate access controls and verification that one customer cannot view another customer’s information. Use an established approach for administrative sign-in and account protection.
  • Confirm operational responsibilities. Document who manages updates, backups and recovery, monitoring, provider accounts, and the response to reported problems.

Clear expectations: Security is a design and operational priority, not an absolute guarantee. Using a provider does not itself establish who is responsible for every possible incident. Provider terms and the project agreement must be reviewed before launch; no transfer of liability is promised here.

Maintenance is separate from adding features

Even when the agreed customer flow stays the same, ongoing technical care must be planned. Website upkeep and provider charges should be explained separately from optional improvements or new functionality.

Area Arrangement to confirm

Routine maintenance Who performs updates, checks, backups/recovery work, and technical support; whether this is a recurring plan or separately scheduled work.

Maintenance pricing and responsibilities remain open. This document does not promise that the website will operate indefinitely without updates, renewals, or ongoing costs.

Preliminary timing

Approximately one month — indicative only

The discovery conversation referenced around 30 days for a basic e-commerce implementation when the scope is settled and materials and feedback arrive promptly. This is not a fixed launch date or a commitment for every feature shown in the prototype.

Confirm the desired launch window around incoming inventory from Nigeria. A practical schedule will depend on agreed scope and payment terms, availability of branding and product content, provider setup, review rounds, and testing.

Confirm scope & budget→Prepare & build→Review & test→Launch & handoff

If the proposed scope exceeds the available budget, agree a smaller launch or later phase before work begins. Any change affecting the approved scope, price, or timeline should be reviewed together first.

06 · Scope boundaries & review

A shared understanding
before we begin.

The prototype is a discussion tool. A visible screen or button does not automatically include every possible integration or workflow behind it.

Not included unless separately agreed

  • Subscriptions or recurring payments
  • Paid recipe access or digital-product sales
  • Marketplace / multiple vendors
  • Loyalty programs or advanced accounts
  • Wholesale portals
  • Complex warehouse management
  • Multi-currency or multi-language
  • Native mobile applications
  • Advanced ERP integrations
  • Custom payment processing
  • Shipping carrier API integrations
  • Complex automated marketing
  • Historical data migration
  • Ongoing ads, SEO campaigns, or content production

Brand identity design, new photography, policy drafting, unlimited revisions, and indefinite support are not assumed. Any extra services, quantities, integrations, or recurring work need an explicit agreement.

How to review the visual attachment

The 23-page visual overview shows the opening views of 18 screens, a payment detail, and selected mobile previews. The optional complete reference shows full desktop layouts. Product and content detail screens represent reusable templates, not a promise of unlimited initial content entry.

The /scope and /prototype screens are presentation aids for this discussion. They are not proposed public production pages. The final customer-facing store name and branding will use the approved business identity.
