Work has started following the initial payment. This milestone prepares the store’s pricing, stock, and order behavior while brand materials, product content, and account access are being gathered.

Built for review

  • Explicit sample variant prices in cents.
  • Server-side cart price and availability checks.
  • Stock reservation, payment matching, cancellation, and fulfillment rules.
  • An interactive owner dashboard with fictional orders and editable sample stock.
  • Content model, product intake template, development log, and screenshot evidence.

Still to connect

  • Client branding, approved products, photos, and policies.
  • Client-owned CMS, transactional database, and owner login.
  • Stripe checkout, signed payment notifications, and order emails.
  • Approved shipping/tax settings, domain, and hosting.
  • Integration testing, final review, training, and launch.

Development preview, not a live store. No real orders or payments are accepted. Owner edits reset on refresh. The tested rules still need transactional storage and verified payment integration before they can handle real purchases.

What the check does

The website reads its own sample prices and stock, validates quantities, and returns a merchandise subtotal. Browser-supplied prices are rejected.

Only product variant IDs and quantities are sent. Contact and shipping fields remain part of the visual checkout preview.

What comes next

Connect real stock and payment services, calculate approved shipping and tax, reserve stock transactionally, and test successful and failed purchases.

Shipping, tax, and the final payable total remain uncalculated. This review does not reserve stock or charge a card.
