On the sales-tax question: **yes, a Texas sales-tax permit is generally required for your Texas-based business to sell taxable merchandise such as the shirts and caps.** It is separate from your IRS tax identification number and LLC registration. The [Texas Comptroller’s permit guidance](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/sales/faq/permit.php) explains the requirements and links to the application. There is no application fee, although a security bond can sometimes be required. Once registered, the business also has ongoing tax-return obligations.

You do not automatically need to register in every other state just because the website ships nationwide. Those requirements depend on where the business has a physical presence and whether sales reach each state’s thresholds. [Streamlined Sales Tax explains the general rules](https://www.streamlinedsalestax.org/for-businesses/remote-seller-faqs/remote-seller-state-guidance).

Food and drink tax treatment can differ from clothing and from one product to another, as the [Texas grocery guidance](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/96-280.php) explains. Please confirm the permit requirements and product tax treatment with the Comptroller or a qualified sales-tax adviser.
